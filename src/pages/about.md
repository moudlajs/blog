Hey, I'm Dan, Cloud Infrastructure engineer by day, fantasy football analyst by night. Based in Brno, I write about infrastructure, cloud, weird bugs that shouldn't exist, and why my waiver wire strategy needs work. This blog is part technical documentation, part sports rant, and entirely influenced by my dog's disapproving stares.

## What's in here

- **Side projects.** Mostly an excuse to learn something else.
- **Fantasy football.** _The Wire_ is set in Baltimore, so when I got into the NFL, the Ravens were the only possible choice. I play on Sleeper.
- **The rest.** Unsorted.

## Why

This is where my own notes, projects and rants live, outside anything with a feed. If something here helps you or makes you laugh, even better.
